How to read this

An event date is when something happened; a published date is when the public learned of it, often weeks later. Most of what happened inside OpenAI was not public until late August. Times and the April to July sequence come from OpenAI’s technical report, which logs events to the minute; the Black Hat talk gives slightly different dates for the lead-up, noted where they differ.
